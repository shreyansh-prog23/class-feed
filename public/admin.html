Subjects
    
Every card is filed under one of these, or under General for whole-class news. To rename a subject, change its name and press Save. It changes everywhere, for everyone. "Also called" is what people type for it, like COA, or its course code.

    

  


  
Groups
    
Say what each group is. A subject group files everything under that subject. The whole-class group and the section groups are read message by message, and each message goes to its subject or to General. A section group also marks its messages for that section only. Groups set to Ignore are not read at all.

    

  


  
Class timetable
    
Set it once here and it shows on every classmate's Today. Upload a photo or PDF of it, check the rows, then save. You can also type or fix rows by hand.

    

    

    

    

  


  
Exam date sheet
    
Upload a date sheet (PDF or photo). Only your class's papers become exam cards. A date sheet posted in a group is read the same way.

    

    

    

  


  
Messages I wasn't sure about 
    
Messages with no date or deadline words are not read, to save AI calls. Press Read this if one matters.

  


  
Import old chats
    
Fill the app with earlier messages. In WhatsApp open the group, tap its name, then Export chat. Choose Include media (a zip) or Without media (a .txt). Upload it here once.
